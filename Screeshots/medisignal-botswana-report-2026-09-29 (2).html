Executive Summary

The national monitoring programme covers 58 health facilities, with data currently available from 55 sites. Across these facilities, 53 medicine‑facility pairs are classified as high risk and 27 pairs as critical risk, indicating an elevated overall risk posture for the supply chain. Of the 55 medicines tracked, a substantial proportion are approaching stock‑out thresholds, necessitating immediate attention from the Ministry of Health.

The most urgent concerns involve three medicines that are already at critical risk and face stock‑out as early as 29 September 2026: Amoxicillin 500 mg, Metformin 500 mg, and Artemether‑Lumefantrine 20/120 mg. Hot‑spot facilities include Kanye Seventh Day Adventist Hospital (Kanye district), Serowe District Hospital (Serowe district), Mookane Health Post (Mookane district), Scottish Livingstone Hospital (Molepolole district) and Shakawe Health Post (Shakawe district), each reporting two critical medicines. Emerging district‑level pressures are evident in Gaborone, Francistown and Lobatse, where multiple facilities report at‑risk stocks of essential treatments.

Priority actions should focus on rapid redistribution and accelerated procurement. Critical redistribution opportunities exist for Oxytocin 10 IU/ml from Nyangabgwe Referral Hospital to Francistown Urban Clinic, Insulin (Soluble) 100 IU/ml from Nyangabgwe Referral Hospital to Princess Marina Hospital, and Amoxicillin 500 mg from Princess Marina Hospital to Kanye Seventh Day Adventist Hospital, each with surplus stocks exceeding 150 days. Concurrently, delayed orders requiring immediate release include Artemether‑Lumefantrine 20/120 mg for Serowe District Hospital (24 days overdue, expected 5 September 2026), Tenofovir/Lamivudine/Dolutegravir 300/300/50 mg for Scottish Livingstone Hospital (21 days overdue, expected 8 September 2026), Insulin (Soluble) 100 IU/ml for Princess Marina Hospital (19 days overdue, expected 10 September 2026), Misoprostol 200 mcg for Mookane Health Post (19 days overdue, expected 10 September 2026) and Paracetamol 500 mg for Gaborone West Clinic (18 days overdue, expected 11 September 2026). Expediting these movements will mitigate imminent stock‑outs and stabilise the supply chain.
